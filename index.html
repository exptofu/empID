Field Marks Glossary
Eye-ring: pale feathers around the eye.
Lower mandible: the color of the lower half of the bill.
Bill size: overall bill length and bulk.
Tail length: tail length relative to the body.
Primary extension: how far the folded wing's primary feathers project beyond the tertials.
Tail width: how broad or narrow the tail looks.
Crown shape: the contour of the top of the head.
Forehead angle: the slope from the bill base to the crown.
Underpart / upperpart contrast: the difference in brightness between the underside and back.
Wingbar contrast: how strongly the wingbars stand out.
Wing panel contrast: how strongly the paler area across the folded wing stands out.
P6 emargination: whether the sixth primary has a notch along its outer edge.
